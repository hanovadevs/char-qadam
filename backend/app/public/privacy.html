Privacy policy

Effective October 31, 2026. This policy describes Stepsy, operated by Hanova Devs.

Data we use

We use your account email, country and timezone, daily step totals and their source, points and voucher transactions, and messages you submit to support. Security and operational logs include IP addresses, request paths, response codes and timing. Leaderboards show other signed-in users your walker identifier, country, step totals and corresponding points. They do not show your email address. Google sign-in sends a verified identity token to our server. Your Google display name and photo may be cached on your device.

Step and health data

With your permission, Stepsy reads steps from your phone sensor, Health Connect or Apple Health. Daily totals, dates and sources are sent to our backend to calculate rewards, show history and rankings, and prevent misuse. Background syncing is best effort. We do not collect GPS routes or sell health data, use it for advertising, or transfer it for credit or lending decisions. Health Connect data is used only for the app features you choose.

Service providers

Our backend runs on our hosting provider and stores app data in Supabase PostgreSQL. Google provides optional sign-in. If you explicitly choose AI help, your submitted question and app FAQ context are sent to OpenAI. We do not automatically send your step history, account profile, or voucher codes to the AI. AI responses are not requested for later retrieval, but provider processing and safety retention may still apply. Use human support for sensitive account issues.

Notifications and device storage

With permission, reminders can include milestones and voucher expiry. Reminder preferences and quiet hours are available in Settings. Background step counting uses a persistent tracking notification with a pause control. Device identifiers reserve an account slot and prevent duplicate rewards. Your device stores authentication securely and caches app data. Camera access is used only when a merchant chooses to scan a voucher; camera images are not uploaded.

Security and your choices

Production access uses HTTPS. You can decline or revoke health and notification permissions in device settings. You can use FAQs and support tickets without AI. Stepsy is a walking rewards app, not a medical service.

Deleting your account

Use You / Profile → Delete account in Stepsy, or request deletion on the web. Profile identifiers, synced step history and support conversations are erased; active vouchers are cancelled and access is revoked. De-identified points, redemption and security records, including device-level daily reward-consumption totals, remain for accounting and prevention of duplicate rewards. These are not linked to the deleted profile. Records held separately by merchants or in your phone's health store are managed by those parties. Deletion applies to active systems immediately; backup copies expire under the hosting provider's backup lifecycle. Contact us for the applicable retention period.

Contact

For privacy, access, correction, or deletion requests, email contact@hanovadevs.com. We may verify ownership before acting on a request.
