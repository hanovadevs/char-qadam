Delete your Stepsy account

You can request deletion of your Stepsy account and associated data without reinstalling the app.

In the app

Open You / Profile → Delete account and confirm.

On the web

Email contact@hanovadevs.com from your account email with the subject “Stepsy account deletion request”. Do not include passwords, payment information or voucher codes. The support team will verify ownership before deletion.

What happens

Profile identifiers, synced step history and support conversations are erased; active vouchers are cancelled and account access is revoked. De-identified points, redemption and device-level reward-consumption records remain for accounting and prevention of duplicate rewards. Your device's health-store data is managed through Health Connect or Apple Health. For the full policy, see Privacy policy.
